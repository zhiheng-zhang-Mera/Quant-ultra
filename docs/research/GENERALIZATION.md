# Generalization and Regime Validation

External validation freezes the source-market research logic, parameter hash, feature version, risk version and execution version before target-market data is evaluated. Target-market search trials must equal zero. Calendar, currency, transaction costs and trading constraints are explicit market context.

Transfer results decompose factor, risk and execution effects and compare strategy versus benchmark return, Sharpe and drawdown. `TRANSFER_SUPPORTED`, `TRANSFER_MIXED` and `TRANSFER_FAILED` are all valid retained outcomes.

Temporal analysis uses predefined, non-overlapping windows. Regime classification covers bull, bear, range, high-volatility and liquidity-shock states; it identifies best and worst environments and audits factor/risk behavior separately.

The frozen US ETF experiment currently supplies an out-of-A-share engineering validation. Its static liquid ETF universe and vendor-adjusted history do not constitute a historical-constituent or investment-validity study.
